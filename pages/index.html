The University of Maryland Strategic Plan

Our Strategic Commitments

The University of Maryland is an extraordinary place—vibrant and dynamic and diverse. We are a place of learning, a school dedicated to the art and science of education and committed to reimagining teaching and learning inside each classroom and community.

We are a campus dedicated to the indisputable value of science and innovation, freely and creatively and rigorously exploring the work of thousands of researchers and discoveries in hundreds of laboratories, libraries, and collaborative spaces. Situated just a few miles from the nation's capital, our university is a place where new knowledge is pursued, discovered, and championed.

We are a community, nearly half a million members strong, of diverse students, faculty, staff, and alumni—a place where thousands come to work every day to make a difference. Our diversity is integral to our excellence, and ours is a community intrinsically connected to our surrounding neighborhoods, our state, our nation, and our world.

Our Vision

We must be united in our noble and fearless purpose. Because celebrating differences and leveling societal inequities must be among our greatest strengths, the grand challenges that define today's ideas and actions are felt not just in individual achievement, but in our collective accomplishments.

Fearlessly Forward in Pursuit of Excellence and Impact for the Public Good, the University of Maryland Strategic Plan presents a bold reimagining of what our university must be—to serve our state, our humanity, and the world.

Our Guiding Principles

To improve the lives of every person on Earth, we will reimagine teaching and learning; accelerate solutions to the grand challenges of our time through creativity and discovery; and forge a diverse, innovative campus community where excellence is celebrated and equity is relentlessly pursued. Together, we will dedicate ourselves to advancing the public good because our individual well-being is enduringly bound to our collective well-being.

This is our time to reinvent our University. We must reenvision our campus and community as a modern flagship research university for the common good.

President Darryl J. Pines
